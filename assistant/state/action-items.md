# Action items

Everything that came out of a meeting recap. Append-only — `/meeting-recap` adds rows here,
and `/meeting-prep` reads them back so the next meeting with the same people knows what's
still open.

Close an item by changing its **Status** to `done` (or `dropped`) by hand. Nothing here is
rewritten automatically.

**Owner `unclear`** means the source didn't say who took it. Those are worth a look — an
action item nobody owns is one nobody does.
